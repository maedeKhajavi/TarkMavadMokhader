Understanding the effects of different substances is key to awareness and prevention.
Here are some commonly known drugs and their impact on health and life

Cannabis

Cannabis, commonly known as marijuana, is a psychoactive plant used recreationally and medically. Its main compound, THC, affects the brain, altering mood, perception, and cognition.

Psychoactive: Alters mood and perception.
Recreational & Medicinal: Used for relaxation and some medical purposes.
Dependency Potential: Can lead to psychological dependence over time.

Heroin

Description: Heroin is an opioid drug derived from morphine. It produces intense euphoria but is highly addictive and can cause severe health issues including overdose and death.

Psychoactive: Alters mood and perception.
Euphoria: Produces intense pleasure, but dangerous.
Health Risks: Overdose, respiratory failure, and long-term organ damage.

Nicotine

Nicotine is a chemical found in tobacco products. It is highly addictive and affects the nervous system, leading to dependence and long-term health risks.

Stimulant: Activates the nervous system.
Highly Addictive: Leads to strong dependence in smokers.
Long-term Risks: Lung disease, heart problems, and other chronic conditions.
